    Why this site exists

    I've spent my working life around data and information systems, but large language models still left me none the wiser.

    So I decided to work it out myself. This site is the result. It obviously isn't the last word on the subject and a specialist would put some of it more precisely than I have or in a more logical order.

    I made this site mainly for friends and family and for anyone else curious to understand the mechanics of LLMs without needing to be a specialist. If it leaves you a bit clearer than before reading then it's done its job. I also take responsibility for any errors and would emphasise this is NOT an authoritative account of how LLMs work, just one person's understanding.

    — John Orosa
